Ask about your date

Questions

Asked before every wedding

How far in advance should we book?
Three to six months for most dates. Autumn weekends in Erbil go first — if your date is set, ask early. I photograph one wedding per date, never two.
Do you photograph in colour or only black and white?
Both, always. You receive the full story in colour, plus a curated black-and-white edit — the signature. You never have to choose.
Do you travel outside Erbil?
Yes — all of Kurdistan and Iraq, and internationally on request. Travel outside Erbil is quoted simply and agreed before booking.
When do we see the photographs?
A small preview within 48 hours — something to share while the day is still warm. The full gallery is delivered within three weeks.
Can our families access the gallery?
Yes. Your private gallery has a simple link and code you can share with family and guests, who can view and order prints themselves.

Tell me about your day.

A date, a venue, what you have in mind — that's enough to start.
