For schools · Erbil

School photography, done properly.

A calm, organised picture day — every class, every student — with portraits parents actually want to buy. One agreement, zero admin for your staff.

The programme

How picture day works

1

We schedule

One planning meeting. We map the day around your timetable — grade by grade, class by class — and send a simple notice for parents.

2

Picture day

Class group photographs and individual portraits, taken quickly and kindly. A calm set, no chaos in the corridors, no lost lesson time.

3

Parents order

Each family receives a private gallery code. They order prints and digital packages directly — delivered back to the school, sorted by class.

Simple for parents, private by design

Galleries are private per student — no public albums, ever. Parents view at home, choose what they love, and pay only for what they order. Photographs of students who don't participate are never taken in the first place.

Not a volume studio

Picture day is led by an internationally published photographer, not a production line. The portrait your students take home should be worth framing — that is the entire point of the programme.

What it costs the school: nothing

The programme is free for the school. Parents buy only what they love — packages start from $10. Schools receive the class group photographs for their own archive, every year.

Questions

What schools ask

What does the programme cost the school?
Nothing. The school provides a room and the timetable; we handle everything else. Parents order only what they want.
How do parents pay?
Directly to us — cash on delivery to the school, or by card/transfer where available. The school never handles money.
What about absent students or retakes?
A short retake session is included in every programme, so absent students and unlucky blinks get a second chance.
How is privacy handled?
Each student's portraits live behind a private code shared only with their family. Nothing is published, and files are stored securely and deleted on an agreed schedule.
