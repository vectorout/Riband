Riband Saadallah

Twelve years of Nordic light.

I learned photography where light is scarce. Twelve years in Finland — Helsinki, Espoo — taught me to wait for it, to work with shadow instead of fighting it, and to keep only the honest frame.

My black-and-white street photography at @bnwlives is followed by thousands. I've served as editor-in-chief of Gilkend, photographed for Suomi Models in Finland, and carried a camera from Rome to Cambodia.

Now I'm home. Riband is the studio I'm building in Erbil — for weddings, portraits, and the city's schools — Nordic restraint, in service of Kurdish celebration.

Black and white is not a filter. It is a way of seeing.

The signature

Every commission is delivered in full colour and in a curated monochrome edit. Colour records the day; black and white keeps the feeling. Fifty years from now, the monochrome frames are the ones on the wall.
